**Status: MEASURED (prior samples only, no model).** Nothing here is in FINDINGS.md yet; 15.3's

Command (worktree at d198055 plus `scripts/prior_properties.py`):

```
env OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 MKL_NUM_THREADS=2 nice -n 15 env -u VIRTUAL_ENV uv run --frozen \
  python scripts/prior_properties.py --n-tasks 1000 --seed S
```

S = 0 (main numbers) and S = 5000 (replicate). Task i uses `default_rng(S + i)`, so the
as-written and the `financial_max_lr_auc: 0.95` arms are paired draw by draw. Prior =
`configs/published/v4-cellattn-labels.yaml` built through `build_prior_config` (max_features
136, n_rows_choices 256/512/1024 drawn per task, p_financial 1.0, sharpness 0.3-12.0). Each
run took about 15-20 s per arm.

## What was measured, and how it differs from §18

- Difficulty: held-out ROC-AUC of `_holdout_lr_auc` (fit on the first half of the rows, score the
  second; median-imputed, standardised). §18 says only "the same logistic-regression pipeline";
  its script is not in the repository, so the statistic may differ in detail.
- Mean |corr|: mean absolute off-diagonal Pearson correlation over non-constant columns after
  median imputation (§18's exact definition is not recorded).
- **Different prior from §18's.** §18 sampled 12 tasks at `max_features=64` from the 2026-09-08
  prior; this is 1000 tasks from the current recipe's prior at `max_features=136` (ratio family,
  sharpness 0.3-12.0, default-rate floor 0.001). Not a like-for-like comparison, and the
  real-panel columns are quoted from §18, not re-measured.
- Held-out AUC can fall below 0.5 (minimum 0.0235) because a half with few positives gives a noisy
  estimate; 94 of 1000 tasks (seed 0) had a single class in a half and are excluded from the AUC row.

Reading, stated as gaps first:

- Difficulty: mean 0.7134 and 0.6977 against 0.7687 real, 0.055 and 0.071 below, a wider gap than
  §19's 0.7429 on its held-out seed. The 0.697-0.899 real range sits inside a distribution that
  reaches both well below chance-adjacent values and up to 1.0; 7.2% (seed 0) and 7.1% (seed 5000)
  of scorable tasks exceed 0.95. Per CLAUDE.md "A prior must span difficulty", the span is deliberate
  and the mean is not a target; §19 used the real mean as a target and §42 reversed that practice.
  Whether this counts as a "lost difficulty match" depends on that policy; this draft does not decide it.
- Width: now spans 4-136 with median 73, against 9-21 in §18; the real panels' 64-95 lie inside
  the interquartile range 39-106. The narrow tail (q05 12-13) remains.
- Correlation: 0.1334 / 0.1346 mean, above the real 0.081-0.110 (median 0.131 also above it);
  the worst tasks reach 0.40. Better than §18's synthetic upper end 0.220 at q95 (0.177-0.181),
  but still too correlated.
- Missingness: mean about 7.7%, against 0-1.5% real: still higher than real, as §18 found.
- Default rate: median 3.3-3.9%, inside the real 3.2-6.9%; the range extends from 0.10% to 29.9%.
