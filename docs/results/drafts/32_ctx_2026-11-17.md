## Reading, within what the partial log supports

- On Polish 1y, `prototype` beats `uniform` in point AP and AUC in 3 of 3 seeds at every context size, and is worse calibrated in ECE (by 0.0023 to 0.0049, still below 0.0100). On 3y the direction is the same but smaller and rests on 1 to 2 seeds.
- `retrieval` beats `prototype` in point AP in 0 of 3 seeds on 1y at every size (the gap narrows from 0.0592 to 0.0168 as context grows), and in AUC in 2 of 3 seeds at each size. `retrieval` has the worst ECE of the five strategies in every cell logged. So the trade on this checkpoint is discrimination for `retrieval` against calibration for `prototype`.
- None of this is tested for significance. The 1y held-out slice has about 81 positives, so a 3-seed point difference of 0.02 AP may be inside the bootstrap interval. No decision on the class default follows from it. §35's finding that the blind strategies are nearly tied on the binary panels is not contradicted by anything here (balanced and hybrid lie between uniform and prototype on 1y; on 3y they sit within about 0.01 AP of uniform).

## Caveats

1. **Incomplete, no record.** 69 of 180 cells, two of four panels, no bootstrap or Holm output, no per-cell JSON (so no `n_context_positive`, Brier, or row-level scores). Taiwan, the one panel with a different data source, is absent, so nothing here speaks to the cross-panel question D9 raised.
2. **Cost mis-estimated.** `retrieval` dominates run time: 1y seed-0 cells took 484 s, 1,378 s and 4,650 s at ctx 500, 1000, 2000 (`run.log`), against 123 to 357 s for the other four strategies. One Polish 1y seed cost roughly 3 h of the 11 h; the grid of 180 cells cannot fit in one 11 h session at this speed. A rerun needs one kernel per panel (`--panels`) or `retrieval` capped below ctx 2000, and the JSON should be written per panel so a timeout does not lose everything. Both are changes to the kernel, not made here.
3. **Not the same setting as 16.1.** The 16.1 draft ran `--n-ensemble 1` on uniform and balanced only; this run uses `--n-ensemble 8`. Values are not comparable across the two without saying so.
4. **Validation, not verification.** The scores are against observed bankruptcy labels on the UCI panels, so this is validation of discrimination on those panels. It is not verification against a solver, and it says nothing about flood work.
5. The feature count (64) is below the checkpoint's maximum, so no cell was skipped for width.
