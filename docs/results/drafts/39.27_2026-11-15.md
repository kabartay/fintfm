# 39.27 draft: the unlabelled two-way cell-attention checkpoint ignores its context labels, so its sub-chance AUC is the sign of a fixed feature function (2026-11-15)

**Status: MEASURED (CPU inference on existing checkpoints, no training), synthetic probes
only.** Nothing here is in FINDINGS.md yet. Worktree at 9621f81 plus
`scripts/diag_39_27_label_independence.py`.

## What was known

- **§91 (seed 0) and §142 (seed 1):** `--cell-labels` off, 6,000 steps, `column_id_dim=16`:
  Bayes-ceiling probe ROC-AUC 0.4575 / 0.4448 / 0.4407 at ceilings 0.900 / 0.990 / 0.999 (seed 1:
  0.4561 / 0.4436 / 0.4399). The labelled arm tracks the ceiling (0.8984 / 0.9882 / 0.9985).
  The unlabelled arm's own in-distribution held-out AUC per task was 0.562 to 0.641 (§91) and
  0.662 at step 6000 (§142), above chance. §91 called the mechanism unexplained and noted that
  the row-within-feature stage lets cells mix context and query rows before any label is injected.
- **§72:** the identity-shuffle checkpoint (`v4-colid-idshuffle.pt`, no cell attention) scores
  0.2988 on `symmetric_sum` and 0.2597 on `symmetric_count` (16 seeds each), a different model
  family and a different probe, also unexplained (its candidate mechanism, extreme derived
  ratios as a spurious cue, is marked untested).
- **§247 (same day):** the two labelled checkpoints read the context labels. True labels reach
  the ceiling and inverted labels give its mirror (Bayes 0.9: 0.899 true, 0.100 inverted).
  §247 did not score the unlabelled checkpoints.

## Candidate explanations

1. **Label-orientation artefact.** The unlabelled model does read context labels (through the
   row-level label embedding that remains after the cell blocks) but with a flipped sign.
   Predicts: inverted context labels lift the AUC above 0.5, and true and shuffled labels differ.
2. **Label-ignoring fixed feature function.** The model's output depends on the query features
   and not on the context labels; the sub-chance AUC is the sign of that function against the
   probe's label direction. Predicts: true, shuffled, inverted, none and unrelated give the same
   AUC; the ranking under true and inverted labels is almost identical; reversing the probe's
   class shift moves the AUC to the other side of 0.5.
3. **Context/query mixing before label injection** (§91's structural observation). Does not by
   itself predict a sign; a mixing artefact that depends on the labels would still show up as a
   difference between label conditions. Not separable from 2 by these measurements, and not
   tested directly (that would need an ablation on the architecture, i.e. training).

## Reading

**Leading explanation (2):** the checkpoint trained without `--cell-labels` ends up with a
function of the query features that is nearly independent of the context labels (rank
correlation 0.99 between predictions under true and inverted labels), with a small
arbitrary-sign slope. A label-free function scores below 0.5 on a probe whose positive
direction opposes its slope and above 0.5 on the mirrored probe (0.55 to 0.60 here); an untrained
network of the same architecture scores 0.21 to 0.30 on the same probes. Sub-chance is therefore
the signature of "no context use", not of a misread context. §91's label "inverted" overstates:
the predictor is a weak fixed function, not an anti-learner.

**Still open:** why training without cell labels produces a label-ignoring model although the
row-level label embedding remains (the training-prior AUC of 0.56 to 0.66 is what a
feature-only function can reach on a prior with a global rule, §47's pattern, untested here);
whether §91's structural observation (cells mix context and query rows before any label) is the
cause, which needs a training ablation and was not done. The §72 inversion is a separate case.

## Caveats

One checkpoint per seed (two seeds); synthetic probes; one task draw per cell in A; magnitudes
in B are milder than §91's. Label-orientation was tested only through the label-condition
columns above; no activation-level analysis.
