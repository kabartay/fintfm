# 48.25: a read-out head trained from scratch on the frozen trunk (2026-11-05)

Draft; not a FINDINGS entry. **PRE-REGISTRATION, written before anything is trained.** Nothing here
has been run on a GPU; the only runs are SMOKE-TESTs of the code on CPU (tiny model, 3 steps).
Costs are ESTIMATED unless marked MEASURED. Peer context: `docs/paper/RELATED_WORK.md`, 2026-11-05
section 4 (CardoAI trained only a label encoder and read-out head on a frozen Nori base).

## The question, and what has to be told apart

§130/§131: a logistic probe on the pre-head representation beats the model's own head on
V4FinBench, +0.039 AP, 5 of 5 folds. §203: on 200 of the prior's own tasks, at **equal labels**,
the head beats the probe (-0.0046 AP, 75 of 200 for the probe). DR3.4 (`--probe-rows context`,
implemented, measurement open) fits the probe on the 2,000 context rows only; if its advantage
falls to 0.005 AP or less the §130-§136 reading is label count, not representation. Three
explanations are live, and 48.25 is designed against the second:

1. **Label count.** The probe saw 20,000 stratified labelled rows, the head 2,000 context rows.
   DR3.4 decides this alone.
2. **The head is the bottleneck.** It was trained jointly with the trunk and is a poor read-out of
   a good representation. Then a head trained afresh on the *frozen* trunk reads the same
   representation better.
3. **A fitted read-out beats an amortised one on real data.** Any head trained on the prior, jointly
   or not, loses live to a per-table fit (§203's reading). Then the new head matches the own head
   and the probe still wins at matched labels.

## What is trained

- **Code.** `fintfm-train --init-from <published checkpoint> --train-only head --reinit-trained`
  (added with this task, default off, byte-identical when off). `--train-only head` sets
  `requires_grad=False` on every parameter outside `FinancialTFM.head` (a `nn.Linear(128, 2)` at
  the published recipe, 258 parameters) and builds the optimiser over the trainable parameters
  only; `--reinit-trained` re-draws that head at its default initialisation after the checkpoint
  loads, so the head is *separately trained*, not the own head plus more tasks. Refused: no
  `--init-from`, with `--resume`, with the hazard objective, an unknown part, `--reinit-trained`
  without `--train-only`. The checkpoint is saved in the normal format; `FinancialTFMClassifier`
  loads it unchanged. A second part, `head_label` (adds `y_proj`, the row-level label encoder,
  closer to CardoAI's "label encoder plus read-out"), exists but is **not** run in this arm: its
  input to the trunk changes, so the trunk is frozen in weights only. It is the follow-up if
  this arm shows explanation 2.
- **Starting weights.** The published `kabartay/fintfm-binary` `v4-cellattn-labels.pt` (SHA-256
  `d231b062...18d2`, the pair `task_dr310_ft_ctl` verifies), downloaded by the kernel and passed
  as `--init-from`. Trunk frozen: column, cell and row encoders, final norm, `y_proj`, readout
  and `row_proj`.
- **Prior and recipe.** The published recipe unchanged (financial prior only, `p_financial` 1.0,
  rows {256, 512, 1024}, lr 3e-4, warmup 500, cosine, seed 0): `configs/arms/48.25-head-only.yaml`.
  Execution flags only: `--amp fp16`, `--loader stream`, single GPU, **masked** attention. Split
  attention is not used because `--init-from` compares `ModelConfig` and the checkpoint stores
  `attn_impl: masked`.
- **Volume: 6,000 steps x 8 = 48,000 tasks.** The cheap screening volume of E1's 48k arms and the
  published checkpoint's own, so the head sees as many tasks as the head it is compared with
  (a head with 258 parameters on 48,000 tasks is not volume-starved; a longer run would only
  move the head away from the matched comparison). If the arm is positive, a 480k volume is the
  follow-up, not part of this pre-registration.
- `--checkpoint-every 1000` is in the kernel; kernel COMMIT is `UNPINNED` and the kernel refuses
  to run until the code is pushed and the generator re-run
  (`scripts/kaggle/train_score/make_train_score.py 4825-headonly-s0` after setting the pin).

## The comparison (all five V4FinBench folds, horizon 0, average precision first)

Run `representation_transfer --probe-rows context` (the cross-fitted DR3.4 protocol: each of 5
folds represented against the other 4/5, probe fitted on the context rows only) **twice, same
folds, same seed**: once on the published checkpoint, once on the new checkpoint. Because the
trunk, `y_proj` and the readout are bit-identical (tested), the probe's representation is the
same in both; the probe arm is therefore one number per fold, and the second run adds the new
head's cross-fitted score (`own_head_crossfit`). Three columns per fold: **own head** (published
checkpoint), **new head** (this arm), **DR3.4 probe at matched context labels**. Mean predicted
rate beside the observed rate for every arm (CLAUDE.md). The kernel also scores the new
checkpoint zero-shot with the packaged inference settings, a cross-check, not the comparison.

## Falsification (the task's Verify, applied as written)

Falsified if the new head's AP is **below the current own head's on at least 3 of the 5 folds**.
Two honesty notes, fixed now. (a) That rule is a sign count: a head that is exactly as good as the
own head falls below it on 3 or more folds half the time, so "not falsified" is not "better".
Both the mean per-fold delta and the per-fold deltas are reported, and a result inside +-0.005
mean AP is labelled "equal", the same threshold DR3.4 uses. (b) One seed; a difference of
this size carries no noise estimate until a second seed is run.

## How the three outcomes read (decided before the numbers)

| new vs own | probe (context) vs own | reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| below on >= 3 of 5 | any | **Falsified.** A head fitted to a frozen trunk does worse than the jointly trained one: trunk and head co-adapt; explanation 2 is out for this head. |
| equal (+-0.005) | within 0.005 | Explanation 1: label count (DR3.4 closes it). The head is not the problem. |
| equal | probe above by > 0.005 | Explanation 3: both heads are amortised read-outs fitted to the prior; the probe's edge is the per-table fit. |
| above by > 0.005 and on >= 3 of 5 | probe above or equal to new | Explanation 2: the joint head under-reads; a separate head recovers (part of) the gap. Next: `head_label`, 480k tasks. |

## Estimated GPU-hours (ESTIMATED, not measured)

Basis: AU5.15's MEASURED live single-GPU rate, 0.80 s/step (`au5.15_2026-11.md`, fp16 split,
checkpoints on, 36,000 tasks/h) and §170's MEASURED fp16 masked 33,959 tasks/h = **0.85 s/step**,
the right row for this arm since it runs masked. Frozen trunk: the forward pass runs in full, the
backward pass stops at the head (no graph is built through the frozen modules since neither their
parameters nor the inputs require gradients), and task generation (about 5% of a step, §170)
is unchanged. If forward is about a third of a full step, a step is about 0.85 x (1/3 + 0.05) =
0.33 s; that split is an assumption, not measured, so the plan uses a range.

| | s/step | 6,000 steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| upper bound: no saving over a full step (AU5.15 / §170 rate) | 0.85 | 1.4 GPU-h |
| frozen trunk, forward about a third of a step | about 0.33 | about 0.55 GPU-h |

Plus five-fold V4FinBench scoring in the kernel, which is the same as every other arm's and has
not been timed for this one; plan for it to dominate. Quota: **about 1 GPU-hour of training
(0.55 to 1.4) plus the scoring**, on one of the two T4s. The first 50 steps of the real run
measure the true rate; the plan above is replaced by it. Memory: the head-only graph is smaller
than a full step's, so the published recipe's footprint is an upper bound (ESTIMATED).
