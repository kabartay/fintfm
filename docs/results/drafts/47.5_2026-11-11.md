# 47.5 unseen-level fractions per dataset (2026-11-11)

**Label: MEASURED on a constructed split, not TabArena's own fold.** Produced by
`scripts/categorical_unseen_report.py --max-context 1000 --seed 0`. The context is a seeded
random sample of at most 1,000 rows (a dataset with fewer than 2,000 rows is split half and
half); every other row is a query. 34 TabArena datasets with at least one categorical column
whose OpenML parquet was already in `~/.openml`; nothing was downloaded.

- `unseen_total`: share of query cells, pooled over the categorical columns, whose level is
  absent from the context. These are the cells `CategoricalTargetEncoder.transform` encodes
  with the prior.
- `unseen_worst_col`: the same share for the single worst column.
- `roc_auc_s101`: mean ROC-AUC the `cat_full` run recorded for the same dataset (binary
  datasets only). It comes from TabArena's own split, so read it beside the fractions, not as
  their consequence. Only Amazon_employee_access (0.8220) was cross-checked against §101.

Reading: four datasets have a pooled unseen share above 0.10 (SDSS17, Amazon_employee_access,
Food_Delivery_Time, kddcup09_appetency), and in kddcup09_appetency and SDSS17 the worst
column is unseen for more than 0.74 of query cells, so the encoder returns the prior for most
of that column there. The pooled figure hides this: kddcup09_appetency pools to 0.1116 over
38 columns. Amazon_employee_access, the largest §101 gain, has 0.6153 of MGR_ID query cells
unseen. This measures coverage only; it does not say how much score those columns carry.
