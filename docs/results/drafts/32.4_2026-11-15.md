## Reading

- The sign is negative: on today's code balanced is above uniform at every horizon (§229 says the
  same; §29's reversed ordering does not reproduce). The prediction was a positive gap
  (uniform ahead) that shrinks with rising rate. Here the gap is negative and its magnitude grows
  with rate, which points away from the prediction.
- That is **not a valid miss** either. The four points are horizons of one portfolio, so rate is
  confounded with horizon length and with AUC level (balanced's advantage tracks how hard the
  horizon is). Rate-by-horizon is not rate-by-portfolio.
- The fold comparison cannot say anything about rate: the span is too narrow.

## What is needed to close 32.4

A binary-path strategy sweep (`fintfm-ctxsweep` or `fintfm-bench` strategy column) that stores
per-cell AUC and the slice's rate, run on panels spanning the default rate (Polish, Taiwan, Home
Credit, V4FinBench single horizon, and ideally one panel near 50%), uniform and balanced at the
same context size, three seeds, rank transform on. That is the same sweep 32.3 still owes, so one
run serves both; the script here would then take its output. Under the no-more-large-campaigns
rule this is a costed request, not something to start. The sweep also needs the plot the Verify
asks for, which this draft does not provide.
