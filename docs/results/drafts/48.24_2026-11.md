# 48.24: majority-downsampled context (draft, 2026-11-05)

Task: `openspec/changes/learn-from-peers/tasks.md` 48.24. Peer source: TabPFN v9.0.0
`SAMPLE_SUBSAMPLING_METHOD="majority_downsample"`, recorded in `docs/paper/RELATED_WORK.md`
(2026-11-05 section). Status: **no new code needed; arm prepared, V4FinBench not run (memory).**

## Does it already exist? Yes: `context_positive_floor` at or above the positive count

`_select_context(strategy="uniform", positive_floor=k)` draws `max(k, round(max_context x pool
rate))` positives, capped at the positives the pool has and at `max_context`, then fills the rest
with a uniform draw of the other rows. With `k` at or above the pool's positive count that is every
positive plus a uniform sample of the negatives up to `max_context`, which is `majority_downsample`
for a binary credit task (the positive class is the last code, the minority on every credit panel
here). The other options do not fit: `balanced` and `hybrid` equalise the classes (50% positive);
`context_positive_cap` only lowers the positive share; `prototype` (AU4.2) undersamples the
majority by k-means to a 0.3 ratio, a different, non-uniform draw; `retrieval` is per query.

Checked by `tests/test_classifier.py::test_majority_downsample_*` (MEASURED, passing):

- Floor = positive count keeps all 47 positives of a 10,000-row pool in a 2,000-row context, the
  other 1,953 slots differ by seed, and a floor of 10^9 selects byte-identical indices to a floor
  of 47.
- **Minority larger than `max_context`: the existing setting degrades to an all-positive context**
  (`max_context` rows of positives, no negative). "Keep every minority row" cannot hold there, and
  a context without negatives carries no boundary. This is the V4FinBench case at the default
  `max_context` of 2,000: 3,587 positives over the panel, about 2,900 per
  training fold (ESTIMATED, 80% of the panel), so the arm must raise `max_context`.
- The base-rate correction reads the context's actual positive rate: a 47/400 context reads as
  11.75% raw and the corrected mean is the pool's 0.47%. Ensemble members, label-swapped ones
  included, take the same path as `context_positive_floor` (`_clone_with_seed` and the member
  positive mapping, tests `test_context_positive_floor_reaches_the_stored_context_and_every_member`).
  Fine-tuning warns for any floor (`context_positive_floor` is in the unseen-by-fine-tuning list).

So no `context_strategy` value or flag is added, and the default path is untouched (floor `None`,
byte-for-byte, `test_context_positive_floor_off_is_the_uniform_draw_byte_for_byte`).

## Prior evidence (read before the arm is run)

§29/§33: balanced contexts (50% positive) cost 6.7-12.3 AUC points on the survival path, and on
V4FinBench a 4,000-row balanced context reached 28% positives (§29, 1,122 positives). The arm
below is about 72% positive, further than balanced from the true 0.36% rate. §200: a floor of 16
(context rate about 0.8%) was +0.0055 AP, null. **Prediction: negative or null.** The mechanism that
could rescue it is that the D5 correction repairs the level; it cannot repair a ranking learned
from a context whose class geometry is unlike the one the checkpoint's 76-921 row training
contexts had.

could not be attributed to composition.
