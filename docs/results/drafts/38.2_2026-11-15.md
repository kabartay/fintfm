# 38.2 draft: which generator property tracks transfer (2026-11-15)

**Status: DRAFT, analysis of recorded results only.** No model was run, trained or scored
for this note. Every number is read from `docs/results/FINDINGS.md` and cited to its section.
This is not a FINDINGS entry and does not close 38.2 (see "What 38.2 still needs").

## Short answer

Of the three properties 38.2 names, **none can be shown to track transfer, and the data
cannot separate them from each other**. On the five checkpoints with a recorded off-domain
score, missingness and correlation structure are two-valued (financial generator against SCM
generator), so they are perfectly collinear with generator family and with the label
mechanism. The one checkpoint that cuts across them (`crossed`: SCM features, financial label)
transfers like the financial arms, not like the SCM arm. That points at the **label mechanism**
(§76, §77), which is not one of the three properties 38.2 lists. Per-column separability goes
the wrong way in the clearest case: `finsharp` is more separable than the SCM prior and
transfers about as badly as the default financial prior.

Five points cannot establish a predictor. The correlations below are descriptive.

## What "off-domain transfer" can mean from recorded results

38.2 and the proposal speak of off-domain performance and mention a non-credit asset-failure
panel (proposal "Why"). **No figure for that panel appears in `docs/results/FINDINGS.md`**
(grep for "asset-failure", "non-credit" and "off-domain" finds nothing relevant), so it is not
used here. Two recorded off-domain measures exist:

1. **Bayes-ceiling probe (§74, §76).** A 6-dimensional Gaussian mean-shift task with an
   exactly known Bayes AUC and no finance content. MEASURED, 10 seeds x 8 column-identity
   draws per point (§74), one checkpoint per row. This is the only measure that covers all
   five checkpoints, and it is the one used for the table. It is synthetic, so it measures
   transfer to a non-financial synthetic task, not to a real non-credit panel.
2. **Polish and Taiwan bankruptcy panels (§75).** Real, but only `fin00` and `fin07` were
   scored: single 70/30 split per dataset at seed 0, 5,910-10,503 rows per dataset (§73),
   untuned. Not usable for correlation (two checkpoints).

For context, the in-domain figure is V4FinBench AP at horizon 0, **fold 0 only, 79 positives,
20,917 rows** (§63): `fin00` 0.0918, `finbal` 0.1900, `fin07` 0.1853. A single fold is not a
result (CLAUDE.md); it is shown only because it is the sole recorded V4FinBench figure for
these arms, and it is not used in any correlation.

## The five checkpoints

`fin10` (pure financial, default envelope), `finbal` (financial, balanced base rate 0.401),
`finsharp` (financial, raised signal-to-noise), `crossed` (SCM features with the financial
label mechanism), `fin00` (pure SCM). §76 also scored `idshuffle` (broken accounting
identities) and §74 scored `fin07` (70/30 mixture); both are left out because no per-property
values are recorded for them in the form needed (idshuffle by design changes the identity
structure, which §76 notes makes it worse, 0.616 at target 0.999).

### Why the raw-AUC correlation misleads

rho = +0.8 on four points reads as "separability helps". The pairwise facts say otherwise:

- `finsharp` has raw AUC 0.8885, above `fin00`'s 0.8306 (§65 says so itself), and transfers at
  0.716 against `fin00`'s 0.984 at Bayes 0.990 (§76).
- Within the financial arms, raw AUC rises 0.7276, 0.7434, 0.8885 while transfer moves
  0.713, 0.714, 0.716 at 0.990, a spread of 0.003 against a raw-AUC spread of 0.16.

So the ordering is carried by `fin00` alone. §77 gives the mechanism: sharpening moves the
median realised task AUC from 0.702 to 0.906 (45 and 47 sampled tasks) but **0 of 92** sampled
financial tasks exceed 0.99, against 17.5% of 57 SCM tasks.

## Which property tracks transfer, and which does not

- **Per-column separability: does not track.** The most separable financial arm transfers like
  the least separable one (§65, §76, §77).
- **Missingness: not distinguishable from generator family.** The 6.8% against 4.4% gap is
  small (§65 called it too small to explain the teaching gap), and `finbal` and `finsharp`
  differ from `fin10` on other properties with no change in transfer, which is what a
  property that does not matter would also look like.
- **Correlation structure: not distinguishable from generator family.** §65 found the
  financial prior *less* correlated by overall measures in one sense (effective rank 25.8
  against 18.3 means more independent dimensions in the financial prior), so the direction is
  the opposite of the "identities make columns redundant" story 38.2's wording suggests.
  `idshuffle`, which breaks the identities, transfers worse (0.616 at target 0.999, §76), not
  better.
- **The consistent split is the label mechanism, not a listed property.** §76: all four capped
  variants share the financial label construction and the one uncapped checkpoint does not;
  §67's `crossed` arm carries SCM-like features and still transfers 0.736 at 0.990. §77's
  bound on realised difficulty supports it. §76 itself records that this was never isolated
  by a trained checkpoint with only the label form varied.

## Caveats

- Five checkpoints, three levels at best, two for most properties. A correlation across five
  points cannot establish a predictor, and these rhos are not offered as evidence of one.
- The transfer measure is a synthetic Gaussian probe. §78 later closed this cap with two-way
  cell attention on the same pure-financial prior (SUPERSEDED note on §74), so the cap is not
  purely a property of the prior. Transfer here is a property of prior plus the architecture
  those checkpoints used.
- No recorded real-domain off-domain score exists for `fin10`, `finbal`, `finsharp` or
  `crossed`. The real-panel contrast (§75) covers `fin00` and `fin07` only, on a single split.
- Property values for `finbal`, `finsharp` and `crossed` are inferred from construction, not
  measured per checkpoint.

## What 38.2 still needs before it can close

The Verify asks for the result to be "recorded as a finding that names which property tracks
transfer and which does not". That means a `docs/results/FINDINGS.md` entry, which this draft is not
(standing instruction: FINDINGS.md not edited here). The proposal text says 38.2 was closed
by §64-§67, but those sections measure what a prior *teaches* (antisymmetric probe), and
§65-§67 name missingness and correlation as unresolved or weak, not as tracking or not
tracking transfer. A later section would have to make the transfer claim, ideally with the
real-panel and per-checkpoint measurements of missingness and effective rank that are
missing here. Gating note for 38.4-38.6: on the recorded evidence, stripping missingness
(38.4) is not supported as the leading candidate, since the three-way collinearity above
means the data cannot favour it, and §76/§77 point at the label mechanism instead.
