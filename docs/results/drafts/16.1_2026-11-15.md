# 16.1 draft: §23's context sweep on the UCI corporate panels (2026-11-15)

Status: draft for the owner. Not in FINDINGS.md (task 16.1's Verify requires numbers there, so the
task stays open). Numbers below are **MEASURED** on held-out rows of the UCI panels, with the
published checkpoint `runs/v4-cellattn-labels.pt`, one command, the checkpoint not trained on any of
these rows.

## Command

```
OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 MKL_NUM_THREADS=2 nice -n 15 uv run --frozen fintfm-binctx \
  --model /Users/morganoko/proj/fintfm/runs/v4-cellattn-labels.pt --out runs/161-binctx \
  --panels polish1,polish3,polish5,taiwan --strategies uniform,balanced \
  --sizes 250,500,2000 --seeds 0,1 --n-ensemble 1 --n-boot 1000
```

Result file: `runs/161-binctx/binary_context_sweep.json` (gitignored). Coverage 48/48 cells and 48/48
comparisons scored, 0 skipped. Wall clock about 55 minutes on a loaded machine (load average above
300 from other jobs), over the 30-minute target: the first launch (3 seeds, 4 sizes) was killed after
two cells and relaunched at this smaller grid.

Harness choice: `fintfm-binctx` covers §23's two strategies (uniform, balanced) and reports AP and
ROC-AUC per cell. It does not reproduce §23's grid of 500/2,000/8,000/20,000 rows: the UCI pools are
far smaller than 8,000, so 250/500/2,000 was used. §23's single-member scoring is matched
(`--n-ensemble 1`).

## Pool, split and slice

Each panel is split 70/30 stratified by seed (`random_state=seed`), no company ids, so the same
split holds for every size and strategy within a seed. Pool = training part of the split.

| panel | rows | training pool | held-out slice default rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Polish 1 year | 7,027 | 4,918 | 3.841% |
| Polish 3 years | 10,503 | 7,352 | 4.729% |
| Polish 5 years | 5,910 | 4,137 | 6.937% |
| Taiwan | 6,819 | 4,773 | 3.226% |

A 2,000-row context is therefore 27% to 48% of the pool, not the 0.75% of §23's Home Credit
(267,000 rows) pool. This is a different regime from §23 and from §162 (V4FinBench, pool about
106,000 rows at the development cap), and it is stated per CLAUDE.md ("`max_context` is a fraction
of the pool").

## Reading

- On the three panels with a base rate near 4% to 7% apart from Polish 1y, a 8x increase in context
  (250 to 2,000 rows) moves AUC by -0.0013, +0.0004 and +0.0099 and AP by -0.0054, +0.0113 and
  +0.0074. That is flat in the sense §23 used, and Taiwan's +0.0099 AUC is the largest of the three.
- Polish 1y is not flat: it peaks at 500 rows (AUC 0.8111, AP 0.2206) and falls at 2,000 rows
  (0.8015, 0.2082), the same shape §162 saw on V4FinBench (AP 0.2215 at 500 rows against 0.1392 at
  2,000 rows, one fold). Balanced removes the dependence on that panel. With 2 seeds this is a
  hint, not a result.
- §162's five-fold full-panel confirmation (500 rows, 1 and 8 members, against the 2,000-row
  default: mean AP change -0.0404 and -0.0029) shows no gain from a context change on V4FinBench.
- Nothing in either corporate panel family shows the monotone rise with context that "context is
  the constraint" would predict. The V4FinBench half is in FINDINGS §162; this is the UCI half.

## Limits of this measurement

- Two seeds, one checkpoint, one scoring member (`n_ensemble 1`). No interval on the context-size
  effect itself: the bootstrap in the harness compares strategies, not sizes.
- Pools of 4,100 to 7,400 rows cap the grid at 2,000 rows. §23's 8,000 and 20,000 rows cannot be
  reproduced here; the question "does more context than 2,000 help" is only answered on V4FinBench
  (§162 stops at 2,000 as well) and on Home Credit (§23).
- Verification of discrimination on observed bankruptcies; no claim about calibration level (the
  slice rate is recorded, mean predicted probability is not tabulated here).
- Over the time target: see Command.

## Recommendation (not a decision)

**Recommend closing the change as not needed on the context-size premise, with the adapter code of
16.2 kept as an option for later.** Evidence: §23 (Home Credit, AUC flat from 500 to 20,000 rows),
§162 (V4FinBench, five folds, no gain from changing context), and this UCI sweep (flat on three of
four panels, a 500-row peak on the fourth). §23's own rule was "if AUC is flat there too, close
rather than build"; the corporate panels are flat to within about 0.01 AUC and 0.011 AP apart from
the Polish 1y peak.

Caveats a reader should weigh before closing: only two seeds; context size is not the only route
by which adaptation could help (domain shift, not context starvation, is what 16.3 would test, and
none of these sweeps measures that); and 16.2 is already built and only 16.3 and 16.4 are open.
Whether to close the whole change is the owner's decision: the task's Verify asks for numbers in
FINDINGS.md and the close-or-build call is a scope decision, so it is not recorded here.
