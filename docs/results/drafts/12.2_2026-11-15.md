# SHAP over gradient boosting on the UCI panels: baseline for attribution (task 12.2), 2026-11-15

**Status: DRAFT for a FINDINGS entry.** Task 12.2's Verify asks for the numbers in
`docs/results/FINDINGS.md`; this draft is not that entry, so 12.2 stays open with a Progress note.

## What was run

LightGBM (200 trees, 15 leaves, learning rate 0.05, subsample and column sampling 0.8, 2 threads)
fitted per stratified fold, 5 folds x 3 seeds (0, 1, 2) per panel. Exact TreeSHAP from
`Booster.predict(pred_contrib=True)` on each held-out fold, in log-odds space. Importance of a
feature is mean |SHAP| over the held-out rows, normalised to sum to 1 per fold. No `shap` package,
no FinTFM model, no tuning.

Command (MEASURED, 2026-11-15, machine load average about 40 at launch):

```
env OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 MKL_NUM_THREADS=2 nice -n 15 env -u VIRTUAL_ENV uv run --frozen --extra bench \
  python src/fintfm/experiments/attribution_baseline.py --seeds 0 1 2 --folds 5
```

It is run as a script because torch and LightGBM segfault together on macOS. Outputs are in
`runs/attribution_baseline/*.json` (gitignored), with the full per-fold importance vectors.

| panel | rows | features | default rate | held-out AUC (mean of 15 folds) | fold-to-fold Spearman | seed-to-seed Spearman | top-10 Jaccard, folds | top-10 Jaccard, seeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Polish 3-year | 10,503 | 64 | 4.71% | 0.9347 | 0.875 | 0.981 | 0.787 | 0.879 |
| Taiwan | 6,819 | 95 | 3.23% | 0.9411 | 0.700 | 0.925 | 0.549 | 0.879 |

Fold-to-fold: pairs of folds within one seed (10 pairs x 3 seeds, importance of one fold's model
against another's). Seed-to-seed: pairs of seeds (3 pairs), each seed's importance averaged over
its 5 folds. Top-10 Jaccard is the overlap of the two top-10 feature sets.

Cost per fold (MEASURED): fit 0.98 s (Polish) and 0.68 s (Taiwan); TreeSHAP 0.45 s and 0.29 s,
about 215 microseconds per explained row on both panels. Wall times carry the load noted above.

Concentration (MEASURED): the ten most important features carry 52.9% of the mean |SHAP| on
Polish and 35.8% on Taiwan. Top features by column index (the loaders give no names), share of
total: Polish x26 10.8%, x33 7.5%, x45 6.0%, x4 6.0%, x12 4.6%; Taiwan x33 5.5%, x67 4.6%,
x39 4.5%, x13 3.7%, x18 3.4%. The Taiwan panel's importance is flatter, which is why its
fold-to-fold ranking is the less stable of the two.

## What it means for 12.4 and 12.5

- The bar to meet is not 1.0. GBM+SHAP disagrees with itself across folds at Spearman 0.70 to
  0.88 and across seeds at 0.93 to 0.98 on these panels. A zero-shot attribution should be
  compared against the seed-averaged GBM importance, and its agreement read against that
  self-agreement ceiling.
- Seed-averaged importance is stable enough to serve as a reference; a single fold's is not on
  Taiwan (top-10 Jaccard 0.549).
- These are verification numbers about one GBM configuration. No claim about explanation quality
  against the real default process: the panels carry no ground-truth attribution, which is the
  gap the prior's ground truth (12.3) is for.

## Caveats

- One untuned LightGBM setting; a different depth or regularisation changes the ranking.
- Correlated ratios split credit among themselves in TreeSHAP; the panels have many near-copies.
- Held-out rows only; AUC here is a sanity check on the fit, not a benchmark arm.
- The synthetic unit test shows the same effect in small: with 4 noise columns, each took about
  5% to 7% of mean |SHAP| (SMOKE-TEST).
