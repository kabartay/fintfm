# DRAFT: diverse evaluation suite, openspec 37.3 and 37.4 (parts b and c of four)

**Status: partial draft, 2026-11-17. Parts a and d have not been fetched. 4 of the 10 cleared datasets are scored.
No verdict on 37.5's gate; no FINDINGS section yet.** Everything below is **MEASURED** (real checkpoint, real
public datasets, 3 stratified folds, one seed) unless marked otherwise. Nothing here is validation of anything
beyond these four datasets.

## Result first (parts b and c, 4 of 10 datasets)

On all four datasets the pretrained checkpoint `v4-cellattn-labels` is below every booster and below logistic
regression on average precision (AP) in three of them. It is above its untrained control on all four, but on
Higgs by 0.0206 AP, which is inside the control's own fold range. Its spread across datasets is wider than the
boosters' (std 0.1510 against 0.0856 to 0.0922 AP, n = 4 datasets, MEASURED), the direction §44 predicted; with
n = 4 and dataset difficulty differing, this is a pointer, not a finding.

Base rates are 0.500 on all four (balanced), so AP and ROC-AUC tell the same story here; AP is listed first
anyway per `CLAUDE.md`.

## Oddities and checks (nothing failed)

- **No failures.** All four datasets: exit 0, `coverage` 18/18 (dataset, fold, arm) units scored, 0 skipped
  (so no `N/M` with M > N; no single-class split, no NaN in any of the 72 per-fold AP and 72 ROC-AUC values read).
  No dataset was skipped for width: the widest scored is MiniBooNE at 50 features.
- **No time limit hit.** Part b: 3,225 s and 1,479 s; part c: 3,823 s and 3,785 s (`summary.json`), against a
  12 h Kaggle limit. The 4-thread Kaggle CPU rate is far faster than the 2-thread local smoke estimate in the
  tasks note (which predicted about 7 to 10 hours in total for all ten on this machine under load).
- **Untrained control is not uniformly at chance.** AP 0.7221 on MagicTelescope (ROC-AUC 0.7195) against 0.4957
  to 0.5456 elsewhere. The control is a randomly initialised network of the same architecture and still ranks
  well above chance on one dataset, so "above untrained control" must be read per dataset, not as proof the
  pretraining contributes; on Higgs the checkpoint exceeds the control by 0.0206 AP while the control's own
  folds span 0.0207. Worth a line in the final write-up and perhaps a check that the control uses the same
  seed on every part (the template seeds it; not re-verified here).
- **Row caps.** Three of four datasets are subsampled to 30,000 rows (a stratified draw with the runner's fixed
  cap seed); Higgs uses 30,000 of 940,160 rows. Boosters could use more rows; the comparison is at equal rows.
  The checkpoint's context is also capped at `max_context` 1,000 rows, so the in-context arms see at most 1,000
  labelled rows however large the training fold, while boosters see the whole training fold (about two thirds of
  the rows used). Both facts bear on the size of the gaps and need stating in the finding.
- **Warnings only.** Each per-dataset log carries three sklearn `OptimizeWarning: Unknown solver options: iprint`
  lines from logistic regression (one per fold); harmless to the result. Kernel logs carry pip resolver notices
  and nbconvert SyntaxWarnings after the work finished.
- **Pinned commit `e475151`** is the head named in both summaries; its commit subject concerns other work
  (multiclass floor and regression arms), so confirm that the `suite_scoring` module at that commit is the one
  the local tests cover before quoting the command as reproduced.
