# 2.2 draft: gradient boosting on levels versus levels+deltas+trends (V4FinBench), 2026-11-15

Draft for a later FINDINGS entry. Not yet in `docs/results/FINDINGS.md`; task 2.2 stays open
for that reason (its Verify names a `bench` variant and a FINDINGS re-derivation).

Label: **MEASURED** (LightGBM only, no FinTFM model, no training). Source file for the numbers:
the run's JSON, reproduced in the table below; the harness is
`src/fintfm/experiments/temporal_gbm_falsification.py`.

## Result first

Adding per-company one-year deltas and a short trend to the levels did **not** improve average
precision. AP was lower with trajectory features on 3 of 3 company-grouped folds
(difference -0.0046 to -0.0156); the paired 95% bootstrap interval excludes zero on fold 1 only.
ROC-AUC was flat to slightly lower (difference -0.00006 to -0.0033, every interval touching or
containing zero).

## Setup (what was measured on)

- Data: V4FinBench company-year panel, pool **1,000,087 rows, 188,338 companies** (full panel).
- Sample used: whole companies drawn at random with seed 0 until the cap, **199,998 rows,
  37,630 companies** (cap 200,000 rows). Histories of sampled companies are complete.
- Label: the loader's `y` = distressed at any of the six horizons h0..h5 (`load_v4finbench`).
  Positive rate in the sample **1.413%** (higher than the 0.19-0.36% per-horizon rates, because
  it is the any-horizon label). Rows near the end of the panel have fewer observed horizons
  (censoring is not corrected here, identically for both arms).
- Features: 136 numeric ratio columns as levels (`levels`); `levels+traj` adds 136 deltas and
  136 trends = 408 columns. Delta = x_t - x_{t-1}, only when the company has a row exactly one
  year earlier. Trend = OLS slope over years t-3..t, at least 3 of 4 points present. Only the
  row's own year and strictly earlier years are used. NaN share: deltas 36.9%, trends 47.2%.
- Folds: 3 company-grouped `StratifiedGroupKFold` (shuffle, seed 0), the project's
  `stratified_splits` recipe; no company in two folds (asserted at run time).
- Model: LightGBM, 200 trees, learning rate 0.05, 15 leaves, subsample 0.8, colsample 0.8,
  `random_state=0`, 2 threads, identical for both arms, no tuning.
- Paired comparison: per-fold difference (traj minus levels) with a paired bootstrap over the
  same test rows, 500 resamples, seeds 0/1/2.

## Verdict, as the proposal pre-registers it

`proposal.md`, "Falsified by": check whether gradient boosting improves when given deltas and
trends versus levels alone; "if the trajectory carries no signal on real data, generating it
synthetically cannot help either."

On this test GBM did **not** improve with deltas and trends: AP lower on 3 of 3 folds, AUC not
higher on any fold. The pre-registered falsification condition is therefore **met on this test**:
no trajectory signal beyond levels was detected by LightGBM on V4FinBench.

Limits on that reading (gaps before capability):

- One seed, one 200k-row company sample (about 20% of the panel), one untuned LightGBM setting.
  Three of the six fold-level intervals include zero; only fold 1 AP excludes it.
- The label covers h0 to h5. At h0 the levels alone nearly solve the task (CLAUDE.md: one column
  gives 0.9799 AUC at horizon 0), which may leave little room for trajectory features. A
  per-horizon split (h3-h5) is not run here and could change the answer.
- 47% of trend and 37% of delta cells are NaN (first years and gaps), which dilutes the extra
  columns.
- This tests the signal in **real** data under gradient boosting. It does not test whether a
  synthetic temporal prior helps FinTFM; that is 2.5.
