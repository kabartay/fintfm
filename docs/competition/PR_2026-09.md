# TabArena submission, September 2026: autogluon/tabarena#616 (closed)

The record of the first TabArena submission: what was submitted, what the review said, and what
was planned for it. The procedure itself, which still applies, is in
[`SUBMISSION.md`](SUBMISSION.md). The resubmission is drafted in
[`PR_2026-11.md`](PR_2026-11.md). This file was `SUBMISSION.md` until 2026-11-19.

## Outcome

The pull request ([autogluon/tabarena#616](https://github.com/autogluon/tabarena/pull/616)) was
made for `fintfm==0.5.5` and reviewed on 2026-09-25. A TabArena collaborator confirmed the
integration was correct and declined the entry on competitiveness: rank 94 of 95 and 0.7823 mean
ROC-AUC over 27 binary datasets. The reviewer also ruled the binary-only coverage ineligible, so a
future submission needs all 51 datasets. We withdrew the submission before the maintainers' full
run, so stages 2 to 5 of the procedure did not take place (`docs/results/FINDINGS.md` §122).

## The sign-off as planned for this entry

These were the checks prepared for stage 3, against the maintainers' full run:

1. **Version** is `fintfm==0.5.5`.
2. **Checkpoint** is the commit above, not `main` and not a local file.
3. **Configuration** is the default alone.
4. **The numbers are in the right region:** roughly 0.78 mean ROC-AUC, near the bottom of the
   board.

The empty search space was to be defended with measurements rather than precedent
(`can_hpo=False` also appears upstream for `causilo`): context size is nearly flat
([FINDINGS](../results/FINDINGS.md) §83/§84), retrieval harms at low prevalence (§70), and
ensembling over column-identity draws is a correctness setting rather than a hyperparameter
([DECISIONS](../design/DECISIONS.md) D12).

All of these were resolved for the resubmission by 2026-11-19: both checkpoints are public
(fintfm 0.12.0), every setting is an entry default with no environment variable, the label is
stated, and the search space is declared empty by the owner's decision
([`PR_2026-11.md`](PR_2026-11.md), FINDINGS §303).
