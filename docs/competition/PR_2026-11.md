## Summary

Adds FinTFM-LinProbe, a new model entry for FinTFM, a tabular foundation model pretrained on a synthetic prior only. It is not zero-shot: a linear readout (logistic regression for classification, ridge or random-Fourier-feature ridge for regression) is fitted on the network's representation of the training rows, one per partition leaf, and also reads the row's own features. It covers all 51 TabArena-Lite datasets with one public checkpoint per problem type and the default config only. Reproduced from this branch on TabArena-Lite it scores **1,022.6 Elo, 108th of 119**. This follows #616 (zero-shot, binary only, declined on competitiveness and coverage).

The method lives in the `fintfm` package (`fintfm.readout`, from 0.13.0); this PR adds a thin AutoGluon adapter, +782 lines in 12 files. An earlier revision of this PR carried the readout inside the wrapper (+7,212 lines); the adapter plus `fintfm==0.13.0` reproduce that revision's predictions bit for bit (details below).

**Benchmark exposure.** The checkpoints were trained on synthetic tables only. The readout's settings were not: every lever of the default config was chosen on the TabArena-Lite fold-0 test splits, and several size thresholds (5,000 and 10,000 training rows) were drawn after seeing those results. The Lite score is therefore development-exposed, each step after the first was inside the Elo interval, and the full run may land lower. Please take this into account when evaluating or listing the entry. [Measurement log](https://github.com/kabartay/fintfm/blob/main/docs/results/FINDINGS.md) (section numbers below refer to it).

**One declared deviation.** `X_val` / `y_val` are not used, but the readout selects its regularisation, its binary cross-fit and its multiclass probability floor by internal cross-validation on the training rows. Details below. We submit the method as measured rather than change it; if you prefer selection on `X_val`, that would be a new measurement.

**Model and pins**

- `fintfm==0.13.0` (PyPI, Apache-2.0, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24357850](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24357850)), pinned in `info.py` and the `fintfm_linprobe` extra (listed in `extended`). No new dependency: `huggingface-hub` is already a tabarena dependency.
- Checkpoints, public and ungated, Apache-2.0, pinned by revision and SHA-256 in `fintfm.readout.checkpoints` and checked on download: binary `kabartay/fintfm-binary@f116bfd43a2b15c65ed3551ea8c38e3364629ddc`, multiclass `kabartay/fintfm-multiclass@b292efa80fbebf759802a980bafe9b5bdcc626bd`, regression `kabartay/fintfm-regression@787a3446685cdcd76eaf079637c50bc4c6fb5a04`.
- Every setting is a per-problem-type default from `fintfm.readout.entry_defaults`, which the adapter's `_set_default_params` reads; no environment variable is needed. `FINTFM_DEVICE` is the only variable read (it forces a device).

**What the default config does** (each a configuration choice on Lite fold 0)

- Partitioned contexts: a shallow tree routes rows to leaves, each leaf has its own context and readout, in bags (4 for binary fits of at most 5,000 rows, 8 for multiclass, 4 for regression).
- Readout inputs: the representation plus the row's squashed features; spline features for binary and multiclass fits of at most 10,000 rows and every regression fit; one-hot indicators for multiclass categorical columns of at most 64 levels; column selection for tables wider than the checkpoint.
- Regression: a random-Fourier-feature ridge for fits of at most 5,000 rows, a ridge otherwise, and predictions clipped to the training target's range widened by 10%. The clip removes a failure, not a tuning gain: on diamonds a few off-manifold rows were predicted near -19 million, and the clip takes its test RMSE from 249,744 to 957, with no table worse by more than 0.1% (§295, §301).

**Declared deviation: internal cross-validation on training rows** (all inside `fintfm.readout`)

1. Binary and multiclass: `LogisticRegressionCV(Cs=np.logspace(-3, 2, 6), cv=3, scoring="neg_log_loss")` picks `C`.
2. Binary: the readout is cross-fitted with 5 folds, so each readout row is represented by a context that leaves it out; categorical encoding is out-of-fold to match.
3. Multiclass: a probability floor is chosen on out-of-fold training predictions.
4. Regression with the random-Fourier-feature readout: `RFFRidgeCV` selects its ridge alpha.
5. Regression: the clip bounds are the training target's range.

None of these reads the validation split; `X_val` / `y_val` and `time_limit` are accepted and unused, as the class docstring states.

**Adapter decisions**

- Device: a granted GPU selects CUDA, otherwise CPU; MPS is never chosen automatically (`_choose_device`; `FINTFM_DEVICE` forces one). `info.py` declares `compute="gpu"`: the nine binary tables above 10,000 training rows were fitted on a T4, the rest on CPU.
- Threads: fit and predict run with BLAS at `min(4, num_cpus)` threads (`blas_threads="auto"`, `threadpoolctl`). Without it, OpenBLAS on an 8-vCPU host oversubscribed and three large tables hit the time limit; with it they finish with identical scores (§296-§299).
- Classes: the multiclass checkpoint represents at most 10 classes, declared as `ag.max_classes = 10`.
- Seed: `seed_name = "random_state"` seeds the row draws, bagging, cross-fit and floor folds.
- `time_limit` does not bound the fit (no training loop); AutoGluon's per-fold projection still applies. On 4-CPU machines QSAR-TID-11 sits near the limit (§301).
- Preprocessing: training and query rows both go through AutoGluon's `preprocess` to one float32 matrix, categorical columns label-encoded, NaN kept; the package does the rest. Imports stay inside the adapter; nothing is patched globally.

**Moving the readout into the package** (§305). Acceptance was set before the move: every prediction equal under `np.array_equal`. The previous revision (6f353cf with `fintfm==0.12.0`) and this one give identical predictions on six synthetic tasks crossing every size gate (from the source tree and from the built wheel), and identical metric error and validation and test predictions on four Lite tables on one machine: credit-g, anneal, healthcare_insurance_expenses and Bioresponse. The Lite results below were produced by the previous revision.

## Tests

Run on this branch with `fintfm==0.13.0` from PyPI (outputs kept):

- `ruff check` and `ruff format --check` (ruff 0.14.0) on the touched files: clean.
- The adapter's tests (`models/fintfm_linprobe/tests/`): 42 passed, 1 skipped (the BLAS-limit test, when threadpoolctl sees no BLAS library). They include a real fit and predict through AutoGluon on CPU for all three problem types, with a small untrained network. The readout's own tests (285) run in the `fintfm` repository.
- `pytest -m models -k FinTFM`: passes. Without CUDA the smoke fit and the warm-up coverage test skip it (`compute="gpu"`); `test_model_many_class` skips it on its declared 10-class limit.
- `python -P -m tabarena.tools.audit_pickle --model FinTFM-LinProbe --problem-type <type>`: "nothing that scales with the training rows sits in model.pkl" for all three types. The unpatched tool crashes on this model: scipy's `BSpline` (in the spline readout) keeps the array-namespace module in `_xp`, `tensor_nbytes` walks into it and through `sys.modules`, and stops on a `urllib3` mapping that refuses iteration. We ran it with module objects skipped in the walk (`isinstance(value, types.ModuleType)`); the same guard would fix the tool for any model holding scipy splines.
- `python -P -m tabarena.tools.audit_transduction --model FinTFM-LinProbe --problem-type <type>`: "every probe row gets the same prediction in every setting" for binary, multiclass and regression (largest difference 8e-7).
- `python -P -m tabarena.tools.audit_warmup --model FinTFM-LinProbe --problem-type <binary|regression>`: warm-up status ok, no failed step, no new packages in the timed fit or predict. The first binary fit loads 20 submodules of `narwhals`, which the warm-up has already imported.
